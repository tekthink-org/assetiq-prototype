What closes a perimeter

A notified boundary is a line on a record. A defended boundary is a line somebody cannot cross without it being obvious. Four things convert one into the other:

  • A physical edge — bund, fence or wall, continuous rather than in stretches.
  • A visible mark — boundary stones at survey-number corners, so the line can be pointed at on the ground and not only on a map.
  • A walked routine — the bank walk on the field screen, at a cadence short enough that a fill is found in week one rather than week twelve.
  • An observation pass — drone or satellite, so the stretches nobody walks are still compared against the baseline.
