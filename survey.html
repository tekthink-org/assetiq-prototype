Why the survey number matters

  • It is how the revenue record is organised. A boundary drawn on a map means little to a tahsildar until it is expressed as a survey number and an extent.
  • It names who has to answer. An encroachment on patta land involves a holder; the same act on government poramboke involves the department that let it happen.
  • It decides the remedy. Removal, regularisation, acquisition and prosecution are different routes, and the classification of the parcel is what selects between them.
  • It survives the argument. A notice that cites a survey number and an extent stands up where a photograph and a map do not.
