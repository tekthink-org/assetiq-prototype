    Geo-fence — why an entry is tied to a place

    An entry from outside the fence is held for the officer, not thrown away. A field assistant sometimes has a good reason, and a record that silently disappears is worse than one that arrives with a question attached.

    Why it is built this way

    • Yes or no, not a percentage. A site engineer answers what they can see. Judgement about progress happens later, from the answers.
    • A photograph for every answer that matters. Anything reported as found must be shown, so a figure can be traced back to the picture that produced it.
    • Location, time and hash at capture. Attached on the device, not typed in afterwards.
    • Inside the fence. A bank walk recorded from the approach road is not a bank walk.
    • Under two minutes. If a round takes longer than the walk itself, field staff stop doing it, and the record dies with them.
