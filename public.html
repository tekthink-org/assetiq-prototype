    The judgement behind this screen

    Publishing the location of an encroachment names a private party in a matter that has not been decided. Publishing an unverified sensor reading turns a suspicion into a headline. Publishing a water quality number invites a comparison with a standard that the number may not have been measured against.

    The default taken here is: aggregate rather than individual, confirmed rather than raised, band rather than raw figure. A citizen can see that the lake is being looked after and can report what they notice. They cannot see who is being proceeded against.

    Not our decision. What a public page shows is the authority's call, not the platform's. This screen exists so the conversation starts from something concrete rather than from a blank page.
